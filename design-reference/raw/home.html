Welcome to TiECON Amsterdam

The meeting point for the people building Europe's next great companies.

TiECON Amsterdam brings together founders, investors, and corporate leaders for a full day of high-signal conversations, investor connections, and partnerships across Europe. It's part of TiE — a global community founded in 1992 that has helped create over $1 trillion in enterprise value.
